Vertical Asymptote (VA)
Q(x) = 0 (بعد التبسيط)
Horizontal Asymptote (HA)
Compare degrees of P and Q
Deg P < Deg Q
HA: y = 0
Deg P = Deg Q
HA: y = (leading coeff P)/(leading coeff Q)
Deg P > Deg Q
No HA (may have slant)
Hole vs VA
